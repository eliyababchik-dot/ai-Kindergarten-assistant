# Staff Scheduling & Management System: Vision

## Motivation & Pain Points

The current situation in early childhood education systems is critical and distressing. Due to last-minute staffing shortages, gaps frequently occur in kindergarten staff assignments, leading to temporary closures. The burden falls heavily on parents, who must miss work and stay home with their children.

Currently, there is no efficient solution to handle sudden assistant cancellations and absences. This creates a long, tedious process of searching for available personnel, including numerous phone calls—often late at night—to find new placements and fill the staffing gap.

## Our Solution

We aim to solve this painful problem using an AI-assisted system that generates up-to-date and suitable assignments based on conversations and mapping of relevant personnel.
